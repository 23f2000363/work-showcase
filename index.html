02 / Engineering principles

Build for clarity and trust.

The most useful software is not only functional; it makes its assumptions visible. I approach product engineering by separating acquisition from interpretation, keeping rules deterministic where possible, and making system state inspectable.

That means respecting source data, validating transformations, testing behavior at boundaries, and designing interfaces around the decisions people actually need to make.

Traceable by designPreserve provenance and distinguish observed data from derived output.
Deterministic corePrefer explicit rules, stable contracts, and reproducible behavior.
Modular pipelinesKeep acquisition, normalization, validation, and presentation distinct.
Useful interfacesMake complex states legible, accessible, and straightforward to operate.
